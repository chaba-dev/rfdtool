////
Text in comment blocks like this one never appears on the site. Read it, then
delete it as you write.

An RFD is read by people who were not in the room, often months later. Write
for them: lead with the conclusion, keep paragraphs short, use plain words,
and define a term before you rely on it.

Goal, Problem, Objectives, and Proposal belong in every RFD. The other
sections are there when they help: rename them, drop them, or add your own.
////

== Goal

////
One short paragraph: what is true once this RFD is done, and for whom. A
reader who stops here should know what the RFD is about.
////

== Problem

////
What is wrong or missing today, and who it affects. Describe how things work
now and why that is not enough, and point to the evidence: an incident, a
request, a measurement, a limit someone hit. Leave the solution for the
Proposal.
////

== Objectives

////
The outcomes that decide whether the proposal worked, as a short list. Prefer
ones a reviewer can check, such as "an agent moves between deployments
without being reconfigured", over ones they can't, such as "better
portability".

Then name the non-goals: what this RFD deliberately leaves out, so that
reviewers don't ask for it and later RFDs know it is still open.

* An outcome a reviewer can check.

Non-goals:

* Something this RFD leaves for later.
////

== Proposal

////
How the objectives are met. Open with the shape of the solution in a
paragraph or a diagram, then give the detail a reviewer needs to judge it,
one subsection per topic: the data model, interfaces, what people see and do,
security, and how it rolls out.

State decisions plainly ("The service stores..."), and give the reason whenever it
isn't obvious.
////

== Alternatives

////
The other approaches you considered, and why this RFD doesn't take them. A
short paragraph each is usually enough.
////

== Supporting material

////
Evidence behind the proposal: data, benchmarks, proof-of-concept results,
how other products solve it, and the issues or discussions that led here.
Summarize each item in a sentence that says what it shows, and link to the
full material rather than pasting it in.
////

== Open questions

////
Questions this RFD hasn't answered yet. As each is settled, write the answer
into the sections above and remove the question. A published RFD has none.
////
